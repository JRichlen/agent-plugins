# Deliverable interface

Write `thesis.md`. Line 1 is `THESIS v0 -> v2: <what changed from the stated thesis, or why the evidence did not move it>`. Then a table `| Cn | verdict | probability | would change my mind |` with one row per claim in `evidence.json`, where verdict is one of `supported`, `unverified`, `mixed`, `refuted`, `unknown` and probability is a number between 0 and 1. Then a `GUARDS:` line naming every claim that is not supported or refuted, and an `UNKNOWNS / DEAD ENDS:` line naming every claim with no evidence. Use only the evidence supplied; do not add evidence or sources.
